Rooftop & Balcony

A Space That Moves With Your Evening

Open-air, flexible, and built for everything from a quiet drink to a full private event.

The Rooftop

Open Air, Open Views

Our rooftop comfortably hosts up to 40 guests, with unobstructed views over Gofa and a relaxed, warmly lit ambiance once the sun goes down. It's the kind of space that works equally well for a quiet coffee or a full evening of celebration.

The Balcony

A Quieter Corner

For smaller groups, our balcony offers a more intimate setting — shaded seating, street-level views of Gofa's daily rhythm, and easy access to the guesthouse below. A natural spot for slow mornings or unwinding after a day out.
