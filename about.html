About Us

Rooted in Gofa

A family-run guesthouse built on hospitality, not just accommodation.

Our Story

Why Dream Pension Exists

Dream Pension started with a simple idea: guests deserve more than a bed and a locked door. Our family opened these doors to give travelers — local and international — a genuinely comfortable place to stay, paired with an honest introduction to Gofa's culture, food, and people. Every detail, from the rooftop to the room amenities, was built around that idea.

Mission

What We're Here to Do

To offer clean, comfortable, and reliable accommodation in Gofa, while giving every guest an authentic connection to the culture, food, and community around them.

Vision

Where We're Headed

To be the guesthouse travelers recommend to friends — not just for the rooms, but for how genuinely it introduced them to Gofa.

Location

Grounded in Gofa

We're situated within easy reach of Gofa's daily life — local eateries, markets, and the community that makes this area what it is. Ask us for directions, recommendations, or a walking route, and we'll happily point you the right way.
